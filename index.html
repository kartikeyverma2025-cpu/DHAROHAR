Gamified Heritage Learning

Transforming historical education into an addictive, bite-sized daily adventure.

The Quest for India's 5,000-Year Legacy

An interactive mobile journey through ancient history, art, and culture designed for students and history enthusiasts.

📜 Traditional Learning

Ancient history and cultural heritage are often trapped in dense textbooks and dry chronological dates. Learners struggle with engagement, retention, and context.

⚔️ Our Game Solution

Bite-sized 5-minute interactive quests. Players unlock ancient empires, master mythology, and earn heritage badges through daily streak-based micro-lessons.

Core Gameplay Mechanics

🔥

Daily Streaks

Build daily learning habits with streak counters and bonus rewards to maintain long-term player retention.

❤️

Praan / Hearts

Players start each quest with 5 Praan hearts. Incorrect answers require careful review, encouraging precision.

🪙

Mudra Currency

Earn Mudra gold coins to unlock historical avatars, custom streak freezes, and rare heritage lore cards.

🗺️

Skill Tree Map

Navigate rich eras from Harappa to the Mauryas, Cholas, and Freedom Era across iconic architectural hubs.

🧩 Interactive Lesson Formats

• Chronology Drag & Drop: Order events sequentially (e.g., Kalinga War to Dandi March).
• Monument Matching: Link world heritage sites to architect rulers (e.g., Tanjore Temple → Raja Raja Chola).
• Inscription Deciphering: Translate ancient Brahmi & Prakrit symbols.
• Map Quests: Identify trade routes, ancient rivers, and kingdom capitals.

🏛️ Digital Heritage Museum

Completing units rewards players with authentic 3D digital artifacts, royal seals, and ancient coins.

Every unlocked item comes with verified archaeological notes by history experts, turning gameplay into a personalized museum collection.
